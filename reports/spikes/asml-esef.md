## What is tagged, and what is not

606 distinct (concept, period, unit, dimension) facts in 640 `ix:nonFraction` elements; 94 `ix:nonNumeric` blocks, all
`ifrs-full` text blocks (notes block-tagged as text, not as numbers). Prefixes on numbers: ifrs-full 105 concepts, asml 24.
No `esef_cor` concept is used at all. Tagged: the four primary statements and a few note totals. Not tagged: every roll-forward
line in Notes 12, 13, 14 (additions, disposals, depreciation by class), the EU Taxonomy KPI tables (pp.199, 201, 203), the
ESRS datapoints. So the twin covers the cash-flow side of T2 and nothing on the KPI side; the KPI numbers have to come from
the report's table, by page, and be tied by arithmetic (T1, T1a) and to the notes (T2a).

Signs: outflows are tagged as positive numbers (the concept's balance says they are outflows) and shown in parentheses by
the layout; 77 facts carry `sign="-"`. A reader must never read the parentheses.

## What the full source (T45) would need

- **Document kind.** `esef`: one iXBRL xhtml per filer-year from filings.xbrl.org (package URL, report URL, JSON URL, index
  `sha256`, `published`, `fxo_id` as the id in place of an accession), plus the same report as PDF from the filer's site when
  a person lists the link in `sources/ir.yaml` (bot check: never fetched by code). Indexes `filings-{YEAR}.json` with
  If-Modified-Since for the watch, as the API docs ask.
- **Parsers.** (1) `ledger/spikes/esef.py` grown into `ledger/reference/esef.py`: facts to the same `{(concept, member, date):
  value}` shape `dimensions.instance_facts` returns, so `reconcile` and the rules take an ESEF twin as they take an EDGAR one
  (`check_method` = `esef:<concept>`, already in `status.METHOD_KINDS`); add `ixt:num-comma-decimal`, `ix:continuation`,
  footnotes, the duration filter (350–380 days). (2) A page-table reader for the untagged tables: the xhtml's `pageView`
  divs give page text with absolute positions (Workiva), so the KPI table and the note roll-forwards can be parsed from the
  xhtml itself without a PDF; the PDF, when a person supplies it, is the second source (`source:<pdf sha>`) and the evidence
  screenshot. (3) The reader agent of T45 reads the rendered KPI page (task: page text as rows, like `render_statement`) and
  answers total / eligible / aligned per KPI with the row and column it cited; code resolves each citation on the page text
  as `extract.resolve` does on a 10-K table.
- **v11 columns that map directly.** `measure` = spent (cash-flow purchases; `category` capex) and a new category per KPI
  row; `standard_concept` = the IFRS concept (`ifrs-full:PurchaseOfPropertyPlantAndEquipmentClassifiedAsInvestingActivities`
  as the us-gaap-equivalent of PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment; `AdditionsOtherThanThroughBusinessCombinations…`
  for the note additions); `reported_name` the line as printed; `source_kind` gains "ESEF tag", "annual report table",
  "annual report note"; `accession` becomes nullable with a sibling `filing_id` (the `fxo_id`) or the column is widened to
  take it; `filed` = `published`; `source_url` = the filings.xbrl.org report URL with `#page=NNN` or the viewer URL with the
  fact id; `source_table` = the page number in place of a parser table id; `unit` gains EUR and `currency` EUR (today both
  are fixed to USD by enum: a one-line enum change, no conversion, no number invented); `check` / `check_method` unchanged.
- **Schema v12 adds.** On the taxonomy row: `kpi_total`, `kpi_eligible`, `kpi_aligned` (whole units, the € amounts; a share
  given only as % stays `share`-style in `kpi_eligible_share`, `kpi_aligned_share`), `kpi_basis` (the Delegated Regulation
  article the filer cites), and the reconciliation items as their own `derived` rows with `relation` and the note page:
  PP&E additions, intangibles additions, ROU additions (empty when not disclosed, `has_value` false), the cash bridge item.
  `rules/`: `kpi_bounds_tie` (aligned ≤ eligible ≤ total, with the rounding tolerance T1b showed), `kpi_parts_tie` (activity
  shares sum to the KPI share), `kpi_definition_tie` (total = sum of the named note additions, open when a part is not
  disclosed), `esef_twin` (statement cell = tag). Period: calendar year; `period_kind` reported.
- **Charter for the reader** (`agents/charters/kpi_reader.md`, before code, T50): owns the KPI rows and the note-additions
  rows for an `esef` document; never touches values, facts, the ESEF twin, `decisions/`; input a task file with the KPI
  page's text rows and the three note roll-forward pages, no tagged value in it; output `agents/schemas/kpi@v1.json`
  (kpi, total, eligible_share, aligned, aligned_share, page, row_label, column_label, cell_text, plus the note additions
  with pages); headless `claude -p`, no tools; judged by the four tie rules above and by citation resolution against the
  page text.
- **Golden fixture shape.** `fixtures/ASML/`: the distilled ESEF facts (`<fxo_id>.facts.json`, same shape as the EDGAR
  distillate), the page-text dump of the pages the reader cites (pp.199–203, 272, 275, 288–292) rather than the 47 MB xhtml,
  the reader's output JSON, the index record; `fixtures/expected/taxonomy.csv` gains ASML's rows with `check` from the
  ties. The full xhtml and the PDF stay out of the repo and are cited by URL and sha256.
- **Estimate.** Parser to the reference shape and its tests: 1 day. Page-text table reader on the xhtml plus the KPI task
  renderer: 1.5 days. Charter, prompt v1, schema, headless run on ASML and the citation resolver: 1.5 days. Schema v12
  columns through store, taxonomy builder, api, publish, release README, dictionary, pages: 1.5 days. Rules and status
  wiring, golden freeze, docs: 1 day. **6.5 builder-days**, in the five-to-seven the estimate in TICKETS F assumed, with

## Risks: what fought back

- **asml.com is behind a bot check.** `https://www.asml.com/en/investors/annual-report/2025` redirects to
  `investor.asml.com/annual-reports/2025` and answers 403 to a plain request; a guessed static PDF path answers 404. Per the
  house rule the spike stopped there. The ESEF xhtml is the same document with the same pagination, so every page cited
  here is checkable against the PDF once a person lists its link; the spike has no PDF hash to record.
- **The KPI table is untagged** in ESEF 2025 (no esef_cor or extension concept). The structured twin does not reach the
  numbers Arctal cares about; those come from a table on a page, so T45 is a table-reading problem with arithmetic ties,
  not a tag lookup.
- **The definition's third part has no figure.** ROU additions are not disclosed in Note 14; the KPI total cannot be rebuilt
  from the notes to the cent (77.3 implied). ASML's own "non-material" treatment of ROU (p.203) is why it does not show.
- **Rounding in the KPI's own shares.** 0.25% is a sum of rounded parts; the amount over the total gives 0.24%. A tie rule
  written as an equality fails on a correct table; it needs a tolerance of one last printed digit per part.
- **Signs and scale live in attributes**, not in the text (parentheses in layout, `sign="-"`, `scale="6"`, `ixt:fixed-zero`
  for a dash). A reader that reads the displayed text gets the wrong sign on 77 facts and the wrong magnitude on all of them.
- **Size.** 47 MB of xhtml, 22 MB of it inline base64 images in CSS; `xml.etree` parses it in 0.2 s, so no lxml needed, but
  the golden fixture must carry the distillate and the page texts, not the file.
- **No accession.** `fxo_id` / LEI replace CIK and accession; three columns in v11 assume the SEC's identifiers.
- **Currency.** EUR throughout; `unit` and `currency` are USD-only enums today.
